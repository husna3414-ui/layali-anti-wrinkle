21

Intended benefits

Depending on the area(s) you have chosen, botulinum toxin type A is intended to:

  • Soften dynamic lines (frown, forehead and crow's-feet lines caused by movement) by relaxing the muscles that create them.
  • Slim the jaw / ease grinding by relaxing an overactive masseter muscle.
  • Lift and smooth the neck & jawline (Nefertiti lift) by relaxing the downward-pulling neck bands.
  • Lift the nasal tip (Tinkerbell) or smooth a pebbled / dimpled chin by relaxing the relevant muscle.
  • Calm excessive sweating by temporarily reducing the nerve signals to the treated sweat glands.

I understand that this treatment cannot remove static lines that are present at rest, and cannot lift significant skin laxity, fat or bone-structure changes.

  • The level of change varies between individuals and depends on my anatomy, muscle strength and the dose used.
  • Results usually start to appear in 2–14 days, and my result will be assessed at around 2 weeks.
  • The effect typically lasts around 3–4 months, after which maintenance treatment is needed to keep the result.
  • No specific result, degree of improvement or duration can be guaranteed.

Contraindications & reasons to postpone

Some conditions, medicines, infections and life stages can make botulinum toxin unsafe, or mean treatment should be postponed — for example pregnancy or breastfeeding, a neuromuscular condition (such as myasthenia gravis), an active infection or skin problem in the treatment area, a recent illness, certain medications, or a known allergy to the product. These are screened for in the medical-history and screening sections above.

Selecting a condition does not automatically mean treatment is impossible. The practitioner will assess whether treatment should proceed, be modified, postponed or declined.

General risks & possible side effects

I understand that possible side effects and complications include:

Common & expected

  • Pain, stinging or discomfort at the injection sites
  • Redness, swelling, tenderness or itching
  • Bleeding, bruising or a small lump
  • Headache
  • Dizziness, nausea, anxiety or fainting
  • Temporary numbness or altered sensation
  • Flu-like symptoms
  • Temporary mild muscle weakness in the treated area

Less common

  • Local infection
  • Uneven, insufficient or excessive effect
  • Under- or over-correction
  • Asymmetry or an altered expression
  • Heavy or lowered brows, drooping eyelid or eyebrow (upper face)
  • Puffy eyelids, dry, irritated or watery eyes (upper face)
  • Changes to the smile or facial movement
  • Persistence of deeper lines at rest
  • Reduced response to future treatments

Rare but serious

  • Severe allergic reaction / anaphylaxis
  • Generalised or excessive muscle weakness
  • Spread of toxin effects away from the injection area
  • Difficulty speaking or slurred speech
  • Difficulty swallowing or choking
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Aspiration or aspiration pneumonia
  • Symptoms of botulism
  • A reaction requiring urgent hospital treatment

Unforeseen risks may also occur. It is not possible to list every possible complication, and rare or unexpected reactions can happen even when treatment is carried out correctly.

25

Masseter treatment — specific risks

Possible effects include temporary chewing weakness or fatigue, difficulty with hard/chewy foods, changes in bite sensation, jaw discomfort, uneven jaw-width reduction, smile/lip asymmetry, temporary weakness of nearby muscles, an unwanted change in facial shape, increased jowls or lower-face laxity, hollowing, paradoxical masseter bulging, and partial or insufficient improvement.

Please confirm all to proceed.
25

Excess sweating treatment — specific risks

Possible effects include pain from multiple injections, bruising/swelling/irritation, incomplete or uneven reduction in sweating, dryness or irritation, altered skin odour, compensatory sweating (increased sweating in untreated areas of the body), temporary weakness of the hand/arm/foot (where treated), temporary difficulty gripping or fine hand movement after palm treatment, reduced response to later treatments, and continued sweating caused by an underlying condition. Results are temporary and typically last around 4–12 months before re-treatment is needed.

Please confirm all to proceed.
25

Nefertiti lift — specific risks

Possible effects include neck weakness or discomfort, difficulty lifting/controlling the lower lip, uneven smile or lower-face asymmetry, facial weakness, difficulty swallowing, voice/speech changes, difficulty controlling neck movement, an uneven jawline, limited improvement where the concern is mainly fat/loose skin/bone, and increased visibility of untreated bands.

Please confirm to continue.
25

Tinkerbell nose — specific risks

Possible effects include limited or no visible improvement, uneven nasal-tip movement, an asymmetrical appearance, an over- or under-elevated tip, temporary weakness flaring the nostrils, changes in upper-lip movement, smile asymmetry, and weakness of nearby facial muscles.

Please confirm all to proceed.
25

Pebbled chin — specific risks

Possible effects include limited or no visible improvement, uneven or asymmetrical chin appearance, chin flattening or hollowing, changes to lower-lip movement or position, a "heavy" or drooping lower lip, difficulty controlling the lower lip when speaking, eating or drinking, temporary speech or pronunciation changes, smile asymmetry, temporary weakness of nearby muscles, and results lasting only 3–6 months. Deeper creases, loose skin and bone/fat structure may not be corrected by this treatment alone.

Please confirm all to proceed.

Alternatives

Reasonable alternatives to this treatment have been discussed with me, which may include:
  • No treatment at all.
  • Delaying and taking more time to decide.
  • Skincare or prescription skin treatment (including clinical-strength antiperspirants for sweating).
  • Other injectable treatments (for example dermal filler or other muscle-relaxing techniques).
  • Energy-based devices (such as radiofrequency, HIFU or laser) where appropriate.
  • Surgery where relevant (for example a surgical lift, rhinoplasty or jaw surgery).
  • Specialist referral — for example a dentist for grinding / jaw pain, or medical investigation for sweating.
  • A second opinion before deciding.

Emergency warning signs — act quickly

Contact Layali Clinic immediately on 07787 708200 if you develop:

  • Severe or rapidly increasing pain
  • White, grey, blue, purple or black skin
  • Blistering or skin breakdown
  • Rapidly increasing swelling
  • Spreading redness or heat
  • Pus or discharge
  • Fever or feeling severely unwell

Call 999 or go to A&E immediately if you develop:

  • Breathing or swallowing difficulty
  • Facial or throat swelling
  • Chest pain
  • Visual disturbance or loss of vision
  • Weakness, confusion, collapse or other neurological symptoms

Acting quickly matters — some complications are time-critical, so please do not wait. Out of hours, if you cannot reach the clinic, call NHS 111, or 999/attend A&E for anything severe or rapidly worsening.

Tell any other healthcare professional (A&E, GP, 111) the treatment you had, the product, the date and the injection/treatment site(s), and show them this consent so they can help you quickly.
